Featured project

Deep, Dark
and Cold

My core PhD research project investigates how Arctic and deep-sea fish survive in extreme environments — high pressure, near-zero temperatures, and total darkness. Most genomic studies focus only on protein-coding genes (≈2% of the genome). This project goes further, exploring the 98% "dark genome" that remains largely unmapped from an evolutionary perspective.

PhD Research Project · 2025–2028

Convergent Genomics of Arctic Marine Taxa

Using comparative genomics, AlphaFold3 protein modelling, and deep learning on non-coding DNA to uncover how unrelated species independently evolve the same survival solutions in the deep ocean. Collaborating with Globe Institute UCPH, Natural History Museum of Denmark, and Greenland Institute of Natural Resources.
