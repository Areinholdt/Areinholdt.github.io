Featured research

Deep, Dark
and Cold

Most genomic studies examine only ~2% of the genome — the part that codes for proteins. This project investigates the other 98%: the non-coding regulatory "dark genome" that controls when and where genes are switched on. The central question: do unrelated Arctic deep-sea fish independently evolve the same genomic solutions to survive extreme pressure, cold, and darkness?

PhD project · 2025–2028 · Active

Convergent Genomics of Arctic Marine Taxa

Combining comparative genomics, AlphaFold3 protein modelling, phyloHMM, and deep learning on non-coding DNA. Generating new chromosome-level assemblies for key Arctic deep-sea species — contributed to the Vertebrate Genomes Project. In collaboration with Globe Institute UCPH, Natural History Museum Denmark, and Greenland Institute of Natural Resources.
